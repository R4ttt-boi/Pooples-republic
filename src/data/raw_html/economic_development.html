Economic Development

This category covers projects aimed at developing the city's economy. Their goal is to improve the quality of urban infrastructure and services and to raise residents' quality of life.

1 - Neighbourhood Economy Model
Why should you cross the whole city for everything when it can be done locally? The project supports the creation of local small markets, community gardens, repair workshops, family services and community enterprises. Residential communities can shape their surroundings based on their own ideas and needs, while new jobs and more accessible services are created. The development strengthens neighbourhood ties and can save residents time and money.

3 - Local Food Supply
By modernising the four existing farmers' markets and expanding their range, fresh local food becomes available in more places. New sales points, refrigeration and storage facilities are built, and an online marketplace enables pre-ordering and local pick-up. Regular gastronomic and family programmes make the markets more attractive. The development increases local producers' revenues, creates new sales opportunities and jobs, reduces food transport needs and makes shopping more convenient for residents.

The development lowers businesses' operating costs and creates new business opportunities and green jobs.

11 - Local Crisis Fund
Local small businesses receive quick financial help when unexpected economic difficulties threaten their operations. The municipality, companies and other supporters set up a joint crisis fund that helps struggling companies with preferential bridging loans, non-repayable grants and business advice. The programme primarily supports smaller shops, service providers and start-ups. Its aim is to preserve jobs, prevent business closures and strengthen the stability of the local economy.

By modernising municipal rental flats and commercial premises, and by involving privately owned properties, new housing and community opportunities are created. The development increases the utilisation of property assets, improves housing conditions, revitalises community life in the neighbourhoods and increases the municipality's rental income.
