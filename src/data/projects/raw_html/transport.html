Environment

This category covers projects aimed at improving the city's environmental sustainability. Their goal is to develop water management, waste management and green spaces, and to improve air quality.

32 - Renewable Energy Park
Kőbánya's former clay pit, the Aknabánya, gets a new role: a 10-hectare energy forest, a solar park, and biogas and landfill gas utilisation facilities are created on the reclaimed site. The complex produces 8,000 MWh of electricity and 6,000 MWh of heat per year, feeding 80% of the electricity into the grid. The development is estimated to reduce CO₂ emissions by 3,000 tonnes per year, while improving the environmental condition of the area.

33 - Renewal of Népliget
One of the city's largest public parks is renewed and becomes a popular venue for sports, recreation and community life. New running tracks, cycle paths, playgrounds and rest areas are built, while green spaces are landscaped and public lighting and public safety are improved. The development increases the park's visitor numbers, encourages outdoor sports and strengthens the area's hospitality and service businesses. The renewed green space improves the urban microclimate, supports biodiversity, and increases the value of surrounding properties and residents' quality of life.

34 - Robert Bosch Campus Expansion
The expansion of Robert Bosch's development campus creates a 90,000 m² innovation centre with modern research laboratories, a vehicle test track and 1,800 jobs. The former industrial site is transformed, and landscaped green areas, walkways, ponds and rest areas are created around the buildings, with more than 200 new trees planted. The campus buildings are linked by new pedestrian connections. The investment strengthens the district's technological role, increases its economic performance and creates a tidier, greener environment.

35 - Richter Centre – New Corporate Headquarters
Richter Gedeon's new 17,400 m², six-storey headquarters is being built on Gyömrői Road, with an investment of around HUF 20 billion. The modern office building expands the company's headquarters with more than 400 workstations, conference and community spaces. As part of the development, landscaped green areas, bicycle racks, electric car chargers and new parking spaces are created. The energy-efficient building modernises the area's townscape, strengthens the district's pharmaceutical and innovation role, and helps retain a highly qualified workforce locally.
