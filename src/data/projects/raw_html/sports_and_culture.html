Sports and Culture

This category covers sports and cultural programs and events supported by the municipality, as well as projects to develop the city's sports facilities. Their goal is to improve residents' health and quality of life and to enrich the city's cultural life.

15 - Utilisation of the Cellar System
The more than 30 km long cellar system of Kőbánya's former limestone quarry holds many opportunities. The project aims to bring this unique underground world back to life: to host concerts, sports and cultural programmes, events and film shoots. The development increases Kőbánya's appeal and visibility, while revenues from programmes and rentals contribute to the maintenance and preservation of the cellar system.

18 - Listed Dreher Villa with a New Function
The renovation of the historic Dreher Villa creates a new cultural and community centre in the heart of the district. The two-storey classicist building hosts exhibitions, concerts, community programmes, conferences and exclusive events. As part of the investment, the villa's façade, interiors and landscaped surroundings are renewed. Revenues from events and room rentals contribute to the long-term maintenance of the building, while the area is filled with new life.

21 - Transport Museum
The new building complex of the Hungarian Museum of Science, Technology and Transport is created by transforming the industrial site of the former Northern Vehicle Repair Works. The investment preserves the valuable industrial buildings and gives them new functions, while tidy public spaces, green areas and new pedestrian connections are created. The cultural centre of national importance attracts visitors and further investments to the area, boosts hospitality and commerce, creates new jobs, and increases the value of surrounding properties and the attractiveness of the district.

22 - Opera House Rehearsal Centre and Workshop House
The abandoned industrial halls of the former Northern Vehicle Repair Works come back to life as the workshop and rehearsal centre of the Hungarian State Opera. The 33,000 m² complex houses a theatre hall, rehearsal rooms, set and costume workshops, and exhibition spaces. The brownfield investment preserves the industrial monuments and also creates a 3-hectare park with a playground, sports and recreation areas. The development creates a new cultural and community destination, provides jobs, attracts visitors and significantly increases the attractiveness of the area.
