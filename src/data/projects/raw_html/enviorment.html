Transport

This category covers projects aimed at developing the city's transport systems. Their goal is to promote sustainable and efficient modes of transport and to reduce traffic congestion.

25 - Bosch Autonomous Vehicle Test Centre
Bosch's new 10,000 m² test track provides opportunities to develop and test the latest technologies of autonomous and electric vehicles. The project builds on cooperation between the company, the municipality, universities and domestic start-ups, creating new research and development opportunities. The investment can attract innovative businesses to the district, while also strengthening local education and training programmes.

29 - Cycle Path Development
A new, continuous cycle route is built along the Rákos stream, connecting Kőbánya with Zugló and District XVII and joining the city's cycle path network. The development provides safer travel for those setting off from the surrounding residential areas to work, school or sports. Rest areas, bicycle racks and landscaped green spaces are created along the route. The investment reduces car use and air pollution, while turning the streamside into an attractive leisure route.
